# /learning-path

Tell the learner where they are on the learning path and what comes next.

1. Read the step list in [`hub/path.mjs`](../../hub/path.mjs) (stages in order, every step is Read, Do or Check). For the reasons behind the order, see [`docs/LEARNING_PATH.md`](../../docs/LEARNING_PATH.md).
2. Ask which step number they are on if you cannot tell (the landing page shows it: "Continue with step N"). Their progress lives in their browser, not in the repo.
3. Answer in short bullets:
   - **Now:** stage and step, in one sentence.
   - **Next three steps:** number, Read / Do / Check, title, link.
   - **Check before moving on:** the next Check step of that stage, as a question.
4. Do not invent extra courses. Extras (last stage) are optional.

For personal Übungen from a teacher, use `/task` instead.
