Stages open one after another. The highlighted step is where you are.

On your computer

Steps marked Do happen on your computer. Get the repository once, then always work in it.

1 · Clone oncegit clone https://github.com/kevin-voss/learning-project.git then cd learning-project. Every page here is a file in the same folder.
2 · DrillsRun make test, or one topic like make variables. Green means done.
3 · ProjectsOpen the project folder and follow its docs/step-by-step.md.
4 · My tasksWork in starter/. Compare with solution/ when you are done. Then commit and push your branch.
