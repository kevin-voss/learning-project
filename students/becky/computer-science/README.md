# Becky · Computer Science (Programmieren)

**Programming 1** (*Programmieren 1*) at Hochschule Hannover. This is the coding subject — not *Einführung in die Informatik*. Technical Informatik (gates, flip-flops, …) lives under [Informatik](../informatik/README.md).

Exercises 01–05 are the weekly sheets of the course, and exercise 90 is Becky's own project *Connect Four*. The task texts come from her course repo [Programmieren-1](https://github.com/bugsbecky/Programmieren-1) and are translated into English here. Every task also gets a picture-first explanation, a desk (`my-solution/`) and a tested sample solution (`solution/`).
