# Becky · Computer Science

Programming 1 at Hochschule Hannover (University of Applied Sciences and Arts Hannover). Everything for this subject lives in this folder.

Exercises 01–04 are the weekly sheets of the course, and exercise 90 is Becky's own project *Connect Four*. The task texts come from her course repo [Programmieren-1](https://github.com/bugsbecky/Programmieren-1) and are translated into English here. Every task also gets a picture-first explanation, a desk (`my-solution/`) and a tested sample solution (`solution/`).
