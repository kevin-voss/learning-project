# Becky · Computer Science

Programmieren 1 (Hochschule Hannover). Everything for this subject lives in this folder.

The Übungen 01–04 are the weekly sheets of the course, and Übung 90 is Becky's own project *4 gewinnt*. The task texts come from her course repo [Programmieren-1](https://github.com/bugsbecky/Programmieren-1). Here every Aufgabe also gets a picture-first explanation, a desk (`meine-loesung/`) and a tested Musterlösung.
