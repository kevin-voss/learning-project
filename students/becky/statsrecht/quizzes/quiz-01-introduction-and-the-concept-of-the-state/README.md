## Words to know

- **constitutional law (Staatsrecht)** — rules on how the state is organised and how citizens relate to it
- **Basic Law (Grundgesetz / GG)** — Germany’s federal constitution; cited with *Art.*, not *§*
- **public law (Öffentliches Recht)** — law where the state is party (Staatsrecht, administration, much of police work)
- **Three-Element Theory (Drei-Elemente-Lehre)** — territory, people, and original state authority together define a state
- **recognition (Anerkennung)** — a political act by other states; not the same as meeting the legal concept of statehood
