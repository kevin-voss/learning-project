Checklistbefore you ship, across the whole sheet

  • Retrieval first: retrieval quality caps generation quality; fix retrieval before blaming the model.
  • Measure before you tune: a golden set turns every change into a fact.
  • Boring plumbing: queues, Postgres and checkpoints beat clever.
  • Meter the output: max_tokens on every call.
  • Validate at the edge: check tool output server-side, with errors the model can act on.
  • Least privilege: scoped tools plus human gates on anything that writes.
  • Filter the exits: block external URLs and images in output.

Try, verify, explain

  1. Choose any two layers. For each, state the system boundary, a likely failure, the first metric or log to inspect and the owner of the control.
  2. Check the "output is the pricey meter" line on your own app: log input tokens, output tokens and max_tokens for 20 requests, price them at your model's rates, and note the output share of cost and how often max_tokens truncates.
  3. Explain why retrieval quality caps generation quality: what does even a perfect model do when top-k misses the answer chunk, and which metric, context recall or faithfulness, exposes it?
